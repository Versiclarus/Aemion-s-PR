# Aemion's P.R - Progress Recorder

A flexible, interactive tracker to track your study progress. Great for students to organise a syllabus, create a practice programme and see their study progression.

Aemion's P.R is a lightweight, browser-based study aid that incorporates random question-paper selection, chapter-wise progress tracking, personalized organizing of studies, and ranking in a gaming framework. With P.R students can monitor what they have solved, where they are weak and stay motivated in their journey.

Features

# Interactive Question Selection

- Two customizable spinning wheels for choosing a question-paper board and year.
- Spin both wheels at the same time and the combination is yours in an instant!
- Spin the wheels by hand to choose the option you want.
- Lock a wheel if you want it to stay locked while the other wheel spins.
- When using the remaining-question filter.
- Select the board and year you will be entering before you update.

# Chapter-Wise Progress Tracking

- Keep notes of your preparations categorized by topics and chapters.
- Track solved question papers using Interactive Board × Year grid.
- Solve or de-select a combination whenever you wish.
- See chapter-level completion counts and progress indicators.
- Store your progress by subject and chapter.

# Progress Analytics

- Insights through heatmaps and progress meters.
- Identify chapter and boards with minimum no of solved question papers.
- Jump in at the most important areas.
- Review recent wheel selections.
- Track your overall progress in study progress and milestones.

# Review-Later List

- Star unfamiliar question-paper combinations for practice.
- Store the starred papers in your own Review Later list.
- View saved combinations by subject and chapter.
- Take out stars when you do not need them any more.

# Personalization and Customization

- Edit student profile & display name.
- Pick among several accent colors and change the appearance of the interface.
- Switch between light and dark themes.
- Personalize wheel name, available options and themes.
- Edit chapter listing to match your books and syllabus.
- Personalize wheels with background images where supported.
- Configure subjects and their individual appearance.

# Language Support

- Bengali and English interface support.
- Keep one language for interface text and another for related content labels.
- Intended to allow access in both languages for students who benefit from knowing them.

# Backup and Data Portability

- Export your progress, wheel setups, presets, and images related to your study to a backup file.
- Upload a backup. To bring back your saved setup.
- Use the exported file to transfer your data to a different device.
- Remove a profile and its data as required.

# Local-First Data Storage

- Your study progress is downloaded to this device.
- No account needed for core tracking workflow.
- Full authority of your data via the app backup and import options.

How It Works

1. Create your Subjects: Choose or tailor your subjects and chapter lists to align with your syllabus.
2. Access the chapter of your choice.
3. Select a question paper: Spin the board and year wheels, and lock any options you want, or select the remaining-questions option.
4. Practice: Attempt the question paper chosen outside the tracker.
5. In the progress grid, check off the Board × Year squared.
6. Self-assess: Progress indicators, heatmaps and weak-spot summaries help you see what needs work.
7. Save documents: Tag Star the combinations you like and want to look at again later.
8. Learn via progression ranks: Advance through the Chapter, Subject, and Lifetime rankings as you log more questions.
9. Back up your data: Periodically save your progress and settings so you can restore or transfer them.

Getting Started

The P.R of Aemion is a complete html app.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Locate the main HTML file.
3. Open in a modern web browser.
4. Organise your subjects and chapters, then begin your practice.

There is no need for a formal install process with the standalone HTML version.

Project Goal

The mission of P.R. of Aemion is to help consolidate study time in an easier manner. Instead of memories to recall which question papers have been solved, students can keep organized logs of their work to discover lapses and develop a more focused study process.

There's a progression to this system-from the beginning of the learning process, all the way to long-term mastery.
